Brownies

This is my favorite recipes.

List of ingredients

  • Flour
  • Nutella
  • Eggs

Steps:

  1. put flour inside a blow
  2. Crack 3 eggs
  3. Pour 250g of nutella in the bowl
  4. Mix it
  5. Pour the mixture into a baking pan
  6. Bake at 175 degrees celsius for 15 minutes
  7. Enjoy!

Porridge

This is one of my favourite food

List of ingredients

  • Yam or potatoes
  • Palm oil
  • Pepper
  • Onion
  • Salt
  • Seasoning Cube

Steps:

  1. Peel and cut the yam/potatoes
  2. Add water and boil until soft
  3. Add Pepper,onion,palm oil,salt,and Seasoning
  4. Cook for 10-15 minutes
  5. Stir and serve HOT!

Yam and eggs

The food that is simple and easy to make

List of ingredients

  • Yam
  • Eggs
  • Oil
  • Onion
  • Pepper
  • Salt

Steps:

  1. Peel and cut the yam
  2. Boil for 15-25 minutes until,then drain.
  3. Fry onion and Pepper in oil
  4. Add beaten eggs,salt and stir.
  5. Serve the eggs with the yam and ENJOY!

Pancakes

My favourite morning breakfast

List of ingredients

  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Butter or oil

Steps:

  1. Mix flour,eggs,milk,sugar and salt in a bowl
  2. Add a little water and mix until smooth
  3. Heat a frying pan and add a kittke oil or butter
  4. Pour in some batter and spread it
  5. Cook both sides until golden brown
  6. Repeat until the batter is finished
  7. SERVE WARM AND ENJOY!

Fried plantain and Eggs

This is the easiest meal to make

List of ingredients

  • Plantain
  • Eggs
  • Oil
  • Onion
  • Pepper
  • Salt

Steps:

  1. Peel and slice the plantain
  2. Fry until golden brown
  3. Beat the eggs with onion,pepper and salt
  4. Fry the eggs
  5. Serve all together!
